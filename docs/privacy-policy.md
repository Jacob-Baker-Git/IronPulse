# IronPulse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 June 2026_

IronPulse is a workout, nutrition, and body tracking app. This policy explains
what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: **everything
stays on your device.**

## Data the app stores

IronPulse stores the information you enter — workout plans, completed
exercises, logged sets, personal records, cardio sessions, body weight,
food log and macro targets, sex (male/female, used for macro and
body calculations), and app preferences (theme, units, reminder time).

All of this is saved **only in the app's private storage on your device**.

## Data the app does NOT collect

- No account or sign-up — the app works entirely offline.
- No data is transmitted to us or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- No access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## Backups

The Export feature creates a zip file of your data that **you** choose where to
send (e.g. your email or cloud drive) via the Android share sheet. The app
never uploads it anywhere on its own. Android's system backup (if you have it
enabled on your device) may also include the app's data according to your
device settings.

## Notifications

If you enable workout reminders or use the rest timer, the app shows local
notifications. These are generated on-device; no notification data leaves your
phone.

## Data deletion

Use **More → Settings → Clear All Data** to permanently delete everything, or
simply uninstall the app — its private storage is removed with it.
